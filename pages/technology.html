Automatic movement

A rotor winds the mainspring as you move your wrist. No battery, and it is the heart of most vintage and luxury pieces.

Quartz & Solar

A quartz crystal keeps time precisely. Light-powered models charge from any light source and run for months in the dark.

Smart sensors

Heart rate, sleep, GPS and notifications on your wrist, with up to 7 days of battery.
