About Us

Alberto Watch Company is a full-service watch repair, appraisal and retail store. Our own Alberto collection is designed in-house, and we service and appraise watches from leading brands too. We look after every piece we sell.

Our Story

Every Watch Has a Story

At Alberto Watch Company, time is more than a measurement — it is a mark of craftsmanship, character, and personal history. From timeless vintage pieces to precision-inspired modern designs, our collection brings together distinctive details, thoughtful design, and the enduring appeal of fine timepieces.
